The composition cluster bootstrap was re-run at B = 1,000 (was 200) with CIs updated consistently across four locations (MS Results −1.3% [−4.8%, +2.0%], MS Discussion, MS statistical conventions, SI Note 8, GUIDE 5.8b); the TCGA sample accounting is now stated as closed arithmetic (3,596 − 3 − 26 = 3,567; pair table spans 3,593 unique barcodes) identically in MS Methods, SI 4.3, and GUIDE 5.10; and the smoking adjustment is now presented as three parallel models with no implied four-covariate joint model.
