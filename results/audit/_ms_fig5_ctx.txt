The PAM50 gradient cannot be read as a functional-divergence ordering: under k_f-only analysis the subtype ordering largely reverses (Luminal A lowest mean k_f, Basal-like highest; Supplementary Note 9), so the \u03c9 gradient is predominantly a baseline effect, and the proliferation-confound explanation (aggressive subtypes proliferate fastest) remains. The raw \u03c9 scale also varies substantially across datasets and computation schemes (e.g., BRCA Luminal A \u03c9 \u2248 142.0 versus mean brain regional \u03c9 = 38.55).
