## Minor issues

**m1 (cosmetic, residue of Round-4 M3). Fig. 1a legend word order misattributes "constrained" to the synonymous baseline.**
The legend now reads: "CKI uses housekeeping gene divergence for k_n — the counterpart of the **constrained synonymous baseline**". As written, "constrained" modifies the synonymous baseline, which (i) is wrong for Ka/Ks (synonymous sites are the *neutral* reference), and (ii) contradicts the immediately preceding sentence of the same legend ("Ka/Ks uses synonymous substitution rate (Ks) as a neutral baseline"). Fix: "k_n — **the constrained counterpart of the synonymous baseline**". No other change needed.

No further conceptual, directional, or correspondence issues from the population-genetics perspective. The fourfold correspondence is now logically self-contained; both NI caveats (denominator-class constraint; polymorphism-class under-counting) are disclosed with correct bias directions; the ascertainment asymmetry on the Ka side remains properly quantified (median 1.61×; leave-pair-out ρ = 0.92–0.94); and the "never as evidence of positive selection" guard is intact.
