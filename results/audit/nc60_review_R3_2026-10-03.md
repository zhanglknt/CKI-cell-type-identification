## Summary

The manuscript introduces CKI, a Ka/Ks-inspired ratio ω = k_f/k_n that normalizes functional (identity-gene) divergence against a housekeeping baseline. My remit is the TCGA application (Result 5): a pan-cancer "reversal" in which normal–normal pairs are more divergent than tumor–tumor pairs across five cancer types (3,535 samples after excluding 32 cell-line-derived aliquots), driven by elevated tumor housekeeping baseline (k_n) rather than reduced functional divergence; a LUAD driver stratification (KRAS > EGFR > WT) that survives purity and smoking adjustment for KRAS but not EGFR; and a GTEx healthy reference arguing the adjacent-normal baseline sits at healthy-tissue levels, i.e. against a field effect.

The TCGA section is, on the whole, unusually careful: ex-CC barcode audit, authoritative linear-normalization re-computation with a softmax sensitivity, cluster-bootstrap CIs as the declared inferential caliber with dyadic-dependence disclaimers, whole-tumor permutation re-fits of the adjusted models (B = 10,000), composition checks at three levels (marker panels, ESTIMATE admixture, reference-free NNLS fallback), and a null survival result. Nearly every number I checked reproduced exactly between main text, supplement, tables, and source JSON. My concerns are (i) the GTEx field-effect argument is presented in the main text more strongly than the supplement's own "tentative" caveat supports; (ii) no batch / tissue-source-site sensitivity for the tumor-specific k_n elevation, which is the central mechanistic claim; (iii) residual confounding in the LUAD driver analysis (TP53, histological subtype, sparse per-tumor pair coverage). I also found a small number of cross-reference and version-string defects from the recent renumbering.

## Strengths

1. **Number discipline is excellent.** Every figure I verified matched across documents: NN/TT ω ratios (LUAD 2.46, KIRC 1.88, LUSC 1.71, BRCA 1.57, LIHC 1.11; Table 12 = Table 2 = main text); k_n TT/NN mean ratios 1.34–3.29 vs. main-text "1.3–3.3-fold"; LUAD group means (136.9/122.2/115.4) and KW P = 7.8 × 10⁻⁷ identical in main text, Tables 13/18, Note 3.13; adjusted log-ω ratio 1.19 [1.12, 1.26] identical in main text and supplement; Cox HR 1.08 [0.88, 1.33], P = 0.467 identical in main text and Table 3; source JSON (`nc52_tcga_excc_summary.json`) reproduces Table 2 point estimates to three decimals (e.g. LUAD 299.76/121.66 = 2.464).
2. **Appropriate inferential hierarchy.** The pair-level MWU P-values are explicitly demoted to descriptive; cluster bootstrap and whole-tumor permutation carry the claims. The anti-conservative fixed-panel TCGA permutation is disclosed and quarantined.
3. **Purity handling is multi-pronged and honestly interpreted.** ESTIMATE admixture adjustment, high-purity-half sensitivity (ratio increases in all five, so admixture can only attenuate the reversal), marker-panel composition check with per-cancer heterogeneity disclosed (LIHC +44%, KIRC +20%), and a reference-free NNLS fallback (ρ = 0.20–0.26). The EGFR association is explicitly and correctly interpreted as an admixture artefact after adjustment (all adjusted P > 0.4), not as a biological null.
4. **Attrition is fully documented.** 3,596 expression-matrix samples → 3,567 pair-level (3 not in pair table, 26 TN-only) → 3,535 ex-CC; pair table 35,306 → 34,828 after dropping 478 CC-touching pairs; mutation-label matching attrition (75/161 labelled → 62/122 matched → 61/120 after double-mutant exclusion) is all traceable (Supp. Methods 5.3).
5. **The ex-CC audit is a model of data-provenance hygiene** (32 ILSBio cell-line aliquots shipped in the LUSC matrix, reassigned by barcode source code, excluded, with superseded pre-exclusion outputs archived).

## Major comments

**M1. The GTEx healthy-reference argument is overstated in the main text relative to the supplement's own caveat.** Main text: "A GTEx healthy reference supplies the missing external comparison… adjacent-normal k_n is as low as healthy k_n (healthy/adjacent ≈ 1.0–1.2)… consistent with tumor-specific elevation rather than a field effect." Supplementary Note 10, however, states that cross-cohort GTEx–adjacent pairs show k_n elevated to tumor levels in every organ (a 1.8–2.3× cross-cohort technical shift) and explicitly labels the healthy ≈ adjacent similarity "**tentative, GTEx cross-cohort discrepancy unresolved**", restricting mechanistic claims to within-cohort orderings. Three further nuances are not surfaced: (a) in liver the healthy/adjacent medians coincide (1.03) but the distributions differ significantly (MWU P = 3.8 × 10⁻⁵, heavier adjacent upper tail), so "as low as healthy" holds only at the median; (b) the claimed healthy/adjacent ratios (1.0–1.2) are *within* the 1.8–2.3× cross-cohort tolerance the authors themselves quantify, so the comparison cannot resolve differences of the magnitude being interpreted; (c) kidney — the exception — has n = 28 with documented autolysis. The main-text Results and the Cover Letter ("arguing against a field-effect reading") should carry the tentative qualifier and state explicitly that the argument rests on within-cohort pair-type orderings bounded by an unresolved cross-cohort shift. As written, a reader of the main text alone would over-rate the strength of the field-effect exclusion. (Locations: main text Result 5, GTEx paragraph; Discussion pan-cancer paragraph; Supp. Note 10 GTEx subsection; CL paragraph 3.)

**M2. No batch / tissue-source-site sensitivity for the tumor-specific k_n elevation — the central mechanistic claim.** The reversal's mechanism is "TT k_n exceeds NN k_n by 1.3–3.3-fold in every cancer type", interpreted as tumor-specific housekeeping dysregulation. But k_n is, by the paper's own design, a detector of technical and physiological drift, and TCGA tumor vs. adjacent-normal specimens differ systematically in procurement path, pathology review, ischemic time, and frequently tissue source site — precisely the cohort-level technical axis along which the authors' own GTEx analysis demonstrates k_n shifts of 1.8–2.3× (i.e., the same order as the claimed 1.3–3.3× biological signal). The composition and purity checks address cell-mixture confounding but not this technical axis. At minimum this needs an explicit Limitations paragraph; ideally a sensitivity (e.g., k_n elevation stratified by plate/TSS where sample sizes allow, or a statement of why such stratification is underpowered) should be added. Without it the "tumor-specific housekeeping elevation" reading — already the load-bearing claim of the GTEx paragraph (M1) — rests on an unexcluded technical alternative. (Location: main text Result 5; Limitations section (iii) covers bulk resolution but not batch/TSS.)

**M3. LUAD driver analysis: residual confounding and sparse per-tumor pair coverage need firmer handling.** (a) Per-tumor ω/k_f/k_n are means over a median of only 4–11 overlapping, seeded-subsampled TT pairs per tumor (2,000-pair cap). The dyadic dependence is disclosed and the whole-tumor permutation (B = 10,000) is the right inferential patch, but the *point estimates* entering the boxplots and group means are noisy averages of as few as ~4 pairs in the largest cohorts; a sensitivity re-computing the driver means at a higher TT cap (or with complete TT pairs for the 492 mutation-labelled tumors, a tractable set) would close this. (b) TP53 co-mutation and histological subtype (invasive mucinous adenocarcinoma is KRAS-enriched) are disclosed as unadjusted — acceptable for an exploratory vignette, but the main-text sentence "KRAS robust to purity and smoking adjustment" (Introduction) reads as a driver-intrinsic claim; since the k_f component (adjusted P = 0.009–0.029) is the only part that could be tumor-intrinsic and it is precisely the part most exposed to subtype confounding, the Introduction and Results should state explicitly that the KRAS association is a bulk tissue-state association with subtype/TP53 confounding unexcluded. (c) Smoking status covers only 87% of tumors and pack-years are unmodeled — disclosed in the supplement but worth one clause in the main text. (Locations: main text Result 5 driver paragraph; Introduction final sentence; Methods "Per-sample divergence"; Supp. Tables 13–17.)

## Minor comments

1. **Stale cross-reference (renumbering miss).** Supplementary Table 14 caption: "Main-text Fig. 5b-d" — the LUAD driver panels are Fig. 4b–d; Fig. 5 is the cross-organ figure. Fix.
2. **Imprecise section citation.** Main text Result 5, first paragraph: the barcode audit is cited to "Section 1.7 of the Supplementary Information", but Section 1.7 is the probability-mapping robustness note; the audit itself is documented in Supplementary Methods 5.3 (cited correctly in Methods) and referenced in Section 3.13/Reproducibility Guide. Point the citation at 5.3 (or both).
3. **Mismatched note citation.** Brain candidate-screen paragraph: "inheriting the four-donor structure (Supplementary Note 13)" — Note 13 is the region glossary; the four-donor design is in Supplementary Methods 5.7 (and Note 11). The later Note 13 citation for region nomenclature (visual-relay/orbitofrontal) is correct.
4. **Version-string inconsistency.** Supplement (permutation-test note in Section 1.5 and the pseudocode note) twice says "the released cki package (v0.5.4)"; the manuscript reports v0.5.5 throughout (Methods, Code availability). Update.
5. **Trivial numeric inconsistency.** Supplementary Table 15 reports LIHC "NN/TT all tumors = 1.10" vs. 1.11 in Table 12 and 1.112 in Table 2 — reconcile (rounding or cohort/floor difference?).
6. **Garbled parenthetical in Section 3.13.** "high-purity-half 1.17 versus 1.19 excluding CC, 95% CI [1.01, 1.44]" — per Table 15 the ex-CC estimate is 1.17 [0.968, 1.412] (CI includes 1), while [1.01, 1.44] appears to be the pre-exclusion CI (excludes 1). As written it is ambiguous which cohort owns which CI, and the two CIs straddle the null differently. Rewrite.
7. **"~84% of the log-ω gap" is not exactly reproducible from displayed values.** From Table 13's rounded group means I obtain ~82% (Δlog k_n = 0.170, Δlog k_f = 0.037). Presumably computed on unrounded per-tumor means — state the inputs or the precise value.
8. **Tone slip in the Introduction.** "…attributable to an elevated housekeeping baseline that GTEx references support as largely tumor-specific" drops the abstract's "appear" hedge and omits the kidney exception; align with the abstract/CL register (the CL itself says "arguing against a field-effect reading", which is fine for a pitch letter but see M1).
9. **Liver nuance.** "adjacent-normal k_n is as low as healthy k_n" holds at the median in liver (ratio 1.03) but the distributions differ (MWU P = 3.8 × 10⁻⁵); one clause in the main text would preempt reviewer confusion.
10. **ESTIMATE re-implementation.** The rank-based single-sample enrichment re-implementation ("monotonically equivalent to published ESTIMATE purity") is acceptable and disclosed, but since purity adjustment is load-bearing for the EGFR-null conclusion, note in Supp. Methods 5.12 whether the re-implementation was benchmarked against published ESTIMATE scores for the same samples.

## Recommendation: Minor revision

The TCGA application is methodologically careful, transparently documented, and numerically consistent to a degree I rarely see; the pan-cancer reversal is a robust tissue-level observation with an honestly bounded interpretation. The required changes are presentational rather than analytical: temper the GTEx field-effect claim to match the supplement's own caveat (M1), acknowledge the batch/TSS alternative for the k_n elevation (M2), tighten the KRAS claim's framing and add a pair-cap sensitivity (M3), and repair the handful of renumbering-induced reference defects. No new large-scale analysis is required for the paper to stand.
