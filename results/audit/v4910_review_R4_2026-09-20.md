# Reviewer 4 (R4) — Cancer Genomics (TCGA / tumor transcriptomics)
**Manuscript:** CKI, omega = k_f/k_n index (v49.10, second round)
**Venue:** Nature Communications (Methodology) — blind review
**Date:** 2026-09-20
**Mandate:** Judge the CURRENT text only. Prior-round flags: (i) bulk-vs-cell-type category mismatch; (ii) undisclosed LUSC→LIHC reassignment of 32 CC samples. v49.10 claims both are addressed.

---

## Summary

The manuscript applies CKI, a Ka/Ks-inspired ratio of a housekeeping-anchored divergence rate (k_n) to an identity-gene divergence rate (k_f), to TCGA bulk RNA-seq (LUAD, LUSC, LIHC, KIRC, BRCA; 3,567 samples) and reports a pan-cancer "reversal" — normal–normal (NN) pairs more divergent than tumor–tumor (TT) pairs in ω (NN/TT 1.10–2.46; bootstrap CI excluding 1 in 4/5 cancers) — driven by a 1.3–3.3-fold elevated TT housekeeping baseline. A LUAD driver stratification survives purity/smoking adjustment for KRAS but not EGFR; LIHC Edmondson and BRCA PAM50 gradients are correctly demoted to "denominator-dominated vignettes"; the LIHC Cox result (HR 1.07 [0.88–1.31], P = 0.48) is demoted to the SI.

The two claimed fixes are **partially addressed only**:
1. The CC provenance fix is a single Methods disclosure sentence. It is factually worded (32 samples, source code CC, ILSBio liver cancer lines, barcode audit, "all counts above reflect this corrected assignment") but is **not accompanied by any sensitivity analysis**, is not repeated in the SI data section, is not flagged at the point of use (Results, Fig. 4), and cites no audit script. Because 8% of the LIHC "tumor" cohort is now cell-line-derived, the LIHC null result — one of the paper's headline calibers ("CI excluding 1 in four of five cancers") — is unvalidated against this artifact.
2. The bulk caveat exists ("ω here quantifies divergence between tissue states rather than between cell types") but the tracked cellular compartment is never named, and the SI's own Hallmark null ("bulk k_f measures broad tissue-state divergence rather than a single tumor-intrinsic program") is in interpretive tension with the main text's "functional component" language for the KRAS contrast.

The manuscript is otherwise unusually candid (denominator-effect disclosures, mapping sensitivity, anti-conservative permutation caveats, batch-confound admission), which I credit. However, the LIHC-specific issues (CC contamination, mean/median k_n incoherence, +33.5% composition attenuation) each independently threaten the only non-significant member of the pan-cancer headline, and none is resolved by analysis in the current text.

## Major concerns

### M1. The CC provenance correction is disclosed but not substantively addressed — a sensitivity analysis is required, not optional

The new Methods sentence (MS, Methods, Datasets, TCGA entry):

> "Sample provenance followed the TCGA barcode sample-source code (positions 14–15): the 32 cell-line-derived LIHC samples (source code CC, ILSBio liver cancer lines) shipped in the LUSC matrix were assigned to LIHC following a barcode audit, and all counts above reflect this corrected assignment."

This is factually complete as a disclosure (32 samples, CC code, ILSBio liver lines, audit) but substantively incomplete in six ways:

1. **No sensitivity analysis.** Nothing in the MS or SI reports LIHC statistics with the 32 CC samples excluded. 32/398 = 8.0% of the LIHC tumor cohort is now immortalized cell lines, not patient tumor tissue. Cell lines are structurally admixture-free, grown under standardized conditions, and have housekeeping-expression profiles unlike any tissue specimen. Whether the LIHC null — "LIHC 1.10 (0.93–1.29)" with "the LIHC CI including 1" — survives their exclusion is unknown and untested. CC–CC pairs with anomalously uniform HK expression would depress mean TT k_n and inflate TT ω, mechanically pushing NN/TT toward 1; conversely CC–tissue pairs could inflate TT k_n. Either direction is an artifact; the reader cannot tell which because the analysis was not run. Given that "four of five" is a headline caliber repeated in the Abstract, Results, and Discussion ("cluster-bootstrap CIs excluding 1 in four of five cancers"), this is a mandatory analysis, not a nicety.
2. **The purity sensitivity is confounded by the same samples.** The claim "restricting tumor–tumor comparisons to the low-admixture (high-purity) half of tumors increased the NN/TT ratio in all five cancer types (LUAD 2.46 → 2.86 …; LIHC 1.10 → 1.17 …)" is advertised as evidence that "The reversal is therefore not an artefact of stromal or immune admixture." But cell lines are by construction extreme-low-admixture samples and will fall in the high-purity half essentially deterministically. The LIHC 1.10 → 1.17 shift cannot be interpreted as a purity effect while CC samples are in the analysis.
3. **The mean/median k_n incoherence may be the CC fingerprint.** The MS reports LIHC mean TT/NN k_n = 1.35 [1.02, 1.88] while the SI reports the LIHC **median** TT/NN k_n ratio = 2.18 (Supplementary Note 8: "median TT/NN k_n ratio 2.18, 2.53, 2.18, 3.70, and 2.79 for LUAD, LUSC, LIHC, KIRC, and BRCA"). A 62% mean–median gap in LIHC — far larger than in the other four cancers (e.g., KIRC 3.29 mean vs 3.70 median) — is exactly what a bimodal TT pair population (cell-line pairs vs tissue pairs) would produce. The text offers no explanation (see M3).
4. **The disclosure does not propagate.** SI Section 4.3 repeats the sample counts — "LUAD (493 tumor + 76 normal), LUSC (534 + 58), LIHC (398 + 57)…" — with no mention of the CC reassignment; the Results paragraph reporting "LIHC 1.10 (0.93–1.29)" and Fig. 4 carry no flag; and the mapping-sensitivity disclosure ("the LIHC effect size is mapping-sensitive and quantitatively weaker under the linear mapping (NN/TT mean ratio 1.10 versus 1.31 under the softmax mapping)") does not consider whether CC samples drive the mapping sensitivity. A correction that lives in one Methods sentence while every downstream consumer of the counts is unflagged is not a substantive fix.
5. **No audit trail.** Every other step cites a notebook ("Scripts: notebooks/nc49_tcga_main.py, …"). The barcode audit that moved 32 samples between cancer types — a cohort-defining operation — cites none.
6. **The barcode claim needs verification.** In the standard TCGA barcode, positions 14–15 encode the two-digit numeric **sample type code** (01 = primary solid tumor, 11 = solid tissue normal, etc.); "CC" is not part of that vocabulary and resembles a GDC "sample source" value (commercial cell line) rather than a barcode-embedded code. The authors should state the exact field audited and release the audit script, or the reproducibility claim is hollow.

**Required:** an LIHC-excluding-CC sensitivity analysis for (a) NN/TT ω ratio and CI, (b) TT/NN k_n mean and median, (c) the low-admixture-half analysis, and (d) the linear-vs-softmax mapping comparison; plus the audit script and corrected SI Section 4.3. Also state whether CC samples entered the Edmondson stratification (n = 288) and the Cox model.

### M2. "The reversal is therefore not an artefact of stromal or immune admixture" overstates what the analyses show, given the authors' own per-cancer attenuation estimates

Results states:

> "per-tumor k_n correlated negatively with admixture in every cancer type (r = −0.23 to −0.42), so admixture can only weaken, not create, the TT k_n elevation—and restricting tumor–tumor comparisons to the low-admixture (high-purity) half of tumors increased the NN/TT ratio in all five cancer types … The reversal is therefore not an artefact of stromal or immune admixture."

The Discussion then concedes:

> "the tumor-pair coefficient attenuates by −0.5% pooled (95% CI [−3.2%, +2.6%]), with marked per-cancer heterogeneity (BRCA −14.0%, LUSC −9.7%, LUAD −2.3%, KIRC +19.6%, LIHC +33.5%). Because marker panels are noisy proxies for true composition, these attenuation estimates are lower bounds on the true composition contribution; the pooled tumor-pair k_n excess is therefore not fully explained by a noisy marker-panel proxy, but neither can a marker-panel regression exclude composition as the driver."

Two problems. First, the categorical Results claim ("not an artefact") is contradicted by the authors' own admission that in LIHC a third (and, per the SI linear update, "LIHC +37.1% [+27.1%, +51.5%]", Note 8) of the tumor-pair k_n coefficient is absorbed by composition covariates — and that this is a **lower bound**. The honest statement is per-cancer: composition is largely excluded for LUAD, largely unexcluded for LIHC and KIRC. Second, the negative-correlation argument (r = −0.23 to −0.42) addresses admixture **level** among tumors but the confound that matters for pair statistics is the composition **difference** between pair members, which the authors themselves show is substantial ("within tumor-tumor pairs k_n correlates with the overall four-panel composition difference (Spearman ρ = 0.387 pooled; 0.23–0.52 per cancer type)"). ESTIMATE-style adjustment also does not capture intra-tumoral epithelial heterogeneity (grade, proliferation), which is the most plausible biological competitor for the k_n signal. The blanket sentence must be rewritten per-cancer, and the LIHC-specific claim withdrawn or supported by a composition-robust analysis (which, per M1, must also be CC-free).

### M3. k_n mean vs median dual reporting remains incoherent, and the discrepancy is largest exactly where the headline result is null

MS Results:

> "in every cancer type the housekeeping baseline was elevated in tumors—TT k_n exceeded NN k_n by 1.3–3.3-fold (mean ratios; 95% CIs excluding 1 in all five cancers, e.g. KIRC 3.29 [2.54, 4.35], LIHC 1.35 [1.02, 1.88]; corresponding median ratios 2.18–3.70 in the Supplementary Information)"

SI Note 8:

> "The reversal replicates exactly (median TT/NN k_n ratio 2.18, 2.53, 2.18, 3.70, and 2.79 for LUAD, LUSC, LIHC, KIRC, and BRCA; all Mann-Whitney P < 10^-90)."

The v49.10 cross-pointer ("corresponding median ratios 2.18–3.70") is a range, not a reconciliation. It leaves the reader with: LIHC mean 1.35 [1.02, 1.88] — barely excluding 1 — versus LIHC median 2.18 — a >2-fold elevation, identical to LUAD. Under the median statistic, LIHC's k_n elevation is unremarkable and the LIHC NN/TT null (1.10) is a numerator/distribution phenomenon rather than a weak housekeeping effect; under the mean statistic, the k_n elevation itself is nearly absent in LIHC. These are different biological stories and the manuscript never says which one it holds. The Methods specify that "Group-level NN/TT and k_n ratios are reported as ratios of means" — so the median is per-pair — but the text nowhere explains why LIHC's per-pair median so far exceeds its ratio-of-means, and no per-pair distribution (or CC-stratified breakdown, M1) is shown. The dual reporting is now cross-referenced but not coherent. Required: per-cancer medians next to the means in the same table, an explicit statement of which statistic each claim rests on, and an explanation (or diagnostic figure) of the LIHC mean–median gap.

### M4. The tracked cellular compartment is never named, and the SI's Hallmark null undercuts the main text's "functional" reading of k_f

The Results caveat is present and welcome:

> "Because bulk RNA-seq averages over tumor, stromal, and immune compartments, ω here quantifies divergence between tissue states rather than between cell types"

But the compartment whose divergence k_f tracks is never identified. Is the intended signal malignant-epithelial-intrinsic? The manuscript's own evidence argues against a clean tumor-intrinsic reading. MS Results:

> "enrichment of the LUAD KRAS tumor–tumor identity panels (7,260 pairs, top-200 genes per pair) against the 50 MSigDB Hallmark programs found no program surviving multiple-testing correction (all q ≥ 0.24), consistent with a distributed identity-gene signal rather than a single-pathway artefact"

SI Section 3.13, same analysis, opposite emphasis:

> "found no program surviving multiple-testing correction (top: Estrogen Response Late, q = 0.24; recurrent panel members are dominated by individually variable, sex-linked and secreted markers), indicating that bulk k_f measures broad tissue-state divergence rather than a single tumor-intrinsic program"

The SI interpretation is the correct one, and it is in direct tension with the MS framing of the KRAS contrast as "a functional component" (Abstract: "KRAS-mutant tumors showed elevated tissue-level divergence whose functional (k_f) and baseline (k_n) components both survived adjustment for tumor purity and smoking"). If the identity panels enriching k_f are "dominated by individually variable, sex-linked and secreted markers," then k_f here is not demonstrably a functional, tumor-intrinsic program — it is a bulk tissue-state statistic that happens to survive ESTIMATE adjustment (which, again, does not adjust for epithelial composition or proliferation fraction). The authors must either (a) name and support the compartment (e.g., epithelial deconvolution, or single-cell-referenced correction), or (b) consistently downgrade "functional component" to "k_f component (unresolved compartment)" throughout the MS, Abstract included. The current split interpretation (MS positive, SI negative) is not acceptable in the same revision.

## Minor concerns

1. **kn_floor contradiction inside the SI.** Supplementary Note 9 states the TCGA severity values are "from the linear-normalization re-analysis at kn_floor = 0", while SI Section 1.7 specifies the linear TCGA pipeline as "kn_floor = 1 × 10⁻⁴, seed 42" and MS Methods states "The TCGA bulk analysis is the sole exception and applies kn_floor = 1 × 10⁻⁴". One of these is wrong; if the severity gradients were really computed without the floor, the pipeline description is not authoritative.
2. **Smoking analysis: LUAD-only, binary, unmodeled dose.** The abstract mentions smoking adjustment, but the analysis is ever/never only ("pack-years were not modeled owing to high missingness and are reported descriptively"; status "427 of 492 tumors, 87%"), and KRAS-mutant tumors are 94% ever-smokers ("94% versus 63% in EGFR-mutant and 86% in wild-type tumors, χ² = 30.3"), which leaves the covariate nearly collinear within group and weakens the adjustment's leverage. No LUSC replication is attempted despite LUSC being in the cohort and the archetypal smoking-associated cancer; a KRAS/smoking-adjacent statement would be far stronger with the LUSC check.
3. **Abstract overstates the biological reading of the k_n elevation.** "driven by a 1.3–3.3-fold elevated housekeeping baseline" is stated as fact, while the Discussion concedes "tumor and adjacent non-tumor aliquots differ systematically in tissue-source site and processing batch (a known TCGA property), so part of the NN/TT contrast could track these technical strata—we did not test a batch-matched subset," and lists "systematic RNA quality differences" as an unresolved alternative. At minimum the Abstract should say "in bulk specimens" and the batch-matched subset test should be performed (TCGA tissue-source-site is available for most aliquots).
4. **Broken/unsupported cross-pointer.** MS Results: "pair-level Mann-Whitney P-values, which ignore dyadic dependence between pairs, are reported as descriptive only in Section 3.13 of the Supplementary Information" — the extracted Section 3.13 contains no such pair-level Mann-Whitney P-values (the only Mann-Whitney values appear in Note 8: "all Mann-Whitney P < 10^-90"). Verify the pointer or move the statistics.
5. **Cox analysis reporting.** The demotion to SI is appropriate given the null (MS: "Cox hazard ratio per SD 1.07, 95% CI 0.88–1.31, P = 0.48, adjusted for stage, grade, age, and sex"), but the n after "listwise deletion of missing covariates" is never stated; report it, and state whether CC samples (which have no survival annotation) entered.
6. **LIHC mapping sensitivity framing.** "the LIHC effect size is mapping-sensitive and quantitatively weaker under the linear mapping (NN/TT mean ratio 1.10 versus 1.31 under the softmax mapping)" is disclosed but framed as a robustness success ("the reversal, its ranking, the k_n elevation … were preserved in direction and rank order"). For LIHC the direction is toward the null and across 1.31 → 1.10 the CI crosses 1; "preserved in rank order" is doing heavy lifting for a result whose significance caliber flips. State plainly that LIHC significance is caliber-dependent.
7. **Cross-checks that pass (for the record):** sample counts sum correctly (3,185 tumor + 382 normal = 3,567) and match between MS Methods and SI 4.3; LUAD driver means (KRAS 136.9 / EGFR 122.2 / WT 115.4; Kruskal-Wallis P = 7.8 × 10⁻⁷) and adjusted contrasts (Δω +16.8, P = 4.0 × 10⁻⁶; joint model +13.6, P = 3.3 × 10⁻⁴) are consistent MS↔SI; Hallmark q ≥ 0.24 matches SI's "top: Estrogen Response Late, q = 0.24"; PAM50 values (Luminal A 142.0 … Normal-like 101.9) match between SI Note 9 and MS Discussion; low-admixture values (LUAD 2.46 → 2.86; LIHC 1.10 → 1.17) match; k_f composition regression (LUAD log gap +0.21; SI range "+0.21 to +0.82, all P ≤ 8.2 × 10⁻⁸⁰") matches.

## Questions to authors

1. What are the LIHC NN/TT ω ratio, TT/NN k_n mean and median, low-admixture-half values, and linear-vs-softmax comparison **after excluding the 32 CC samples**? Did the pre-correction LUSC counts include these samples, and did any LUSC result change?
2. Which exact TCGA/GDC field encodes "CC", at which barcode positions? Please deposit the barcode-audit script alongside the other notebooks.
3. Please explain the LIHC k_n mean (1.35) vs median (2.18) divergence and show the per-pair ratio distribution (ideally CC-stratified).
4. Which cellular compartment do you claim the TCGA k_f signal tracks, and what evidence (deconvolution, epithelial markers, single-cell reference) supports that attribution? Is the SI's "broad tissue-state divergence rather than a single tumor-intrinsic program" the intended reading of the Hallmark null, and if so, why does the MS call the KRAS k_f component "functional"?
5. Is Supplementary Note 9's "kn_floor = 0" a typo for the 1 × 10⁻⁴ used everywhere else, or was the severity analysis genuinely run unfloored?
6. Did any of the 32 CC samples enter the LIHC Edmondson stratification, the Cox model, or the ESTIMATE scoring (the rank-based scoring "over all 45,504 expressed genes of the five-cancer merged matrix" is affected by the sample set composition)?
7. Why was no batch/tissue-source-site-matched NN/TT subset tested, given the Discussion's own admission that this stratum could carry part of the contrast?

## Score: 5/10

## Recommendation: **Major Revision**

The prior-round fixes are present as sentences but not as analyses. The CC correction moves 8% of the LIHC cohort without a sensitivity analysis, while the LIHC null is a headline caliber; the purity claim overreaches the authors' own per-cancer attenuation estimates; the mean/median k_n incoherence is unresolved precisely in LIHC; and the MS/SI disagree on what the Hallmark null means for the "functional" label on k_f. All are addressable with existing data and modest compute, but they require new analysis, not prose.
