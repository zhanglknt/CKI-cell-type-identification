## Summary

The manuscript introduces CKI, a per-comparison divergence index that normalizes identity-gene divergence (k_f) against a housekeeping-gene baseline (k_n), and validates it across simulation, technical replicates, five TCGA cancer types, the Tabula Sapiens cross-organ atlas, and the Siletti brain atlas. The submission package is, in my assessment, close to submission-ready for Nature Communications: the abstract is 168 words, citation-free, and four-pillar; the main text (Introduction + Results + Discussion) is ~4,994 words, inside NC's ~5,000-word guidance; the section architecture follows NC order (Abstract → Introduction → Results → Discussion → Methods → Data availability → Code availability → References → Acknowledgements → Author contributions → Competing interests), with no Conclusions section and no keywords; the required LLM-use declaration is present; and Data/Code availability are complete with accessions, a GitHub tag, and Zenodo DOIs.

My verification of the newly renumbered supplementary apparatus confirms that the global first-citation order is now monotonic: Supplementary Notes 1–16 are first cited in the main text in strict numerical order (lines 14, 27, 27, 27, 31, 33, 40, 42, 42, 46, 53, 55, 60, 64, 71, 85 of the extracted text), Supplementary Figs. 1–14 likewise (lines 20, 27, 40, 42, 44, 49, 52, 55, 61, 62, 63, 73, 85, 116), and Supplementary Tables follow a consistent global scheme — Tables 1–6 are first cited in the main text in order (lines 22, 46, 46, 50, 52, 62), and Tables 7–19 are first cited inside the SI in strict sequential order (SI body lines 125, 126, 127, 131, 132, 135, 136, 136, 136, 137, 137, 176, 204). The renumbering is therefore internally correct. The residual problems are presentational and navigational, not numerical.

## Strengths

1. **Scope fit is genuine, not asserted.** NC's methods-with-broad-application niche is well matched: the method is validated on four biologically distinct arenas (simulation, drift calibration, pan-cancer bulk, brain atlas), and the two applied results (the NN/TT pan-cancer reversal; the regional differentiation gradient) give the general readership something beyond the algorithm.
2. **Novelty framing is disciplined and defensible.** The cover letter's claim ("to our knowledge, the first per-comparison, design-testable implementation of a principled separation of functional divergence from background drift") is narrow, falsifiable, and explicitly scoped — and the Discussion itself pre-empts the novelty attack by stating CKI "is better described as a recombination of established ideas than a conceptual shift" with citations to the normalization, housekeeping-genomics, and noise literatures (refs 29–33). This is exactly the calibration NC editors look for; the claim can be defended in rebuttal.
3. **Abstract quality.** 168 words, no citations, all four pillars stated with quantitative anchors (AUC 0.80; 3,535 samples; gradient), limitations implicitly bounded ("provided a statistically calibrated framework"). Fully independent of the main text.
4. **Reference apparatus.** 57 references, numbered in order of first citation (verified monotonic 1→57 across Introduction → Methods), Nature style consistently applied (all authors listed when ≤5, first-author et al. when >5; consortia named corporately).
5. **Main-text figure legends are genuinely self-contained.** Fig. 2e, Fig. 3, Fig. 4, and Fig. 6 legends carry n, tests, CIs, and interpretive caveats (e.g., Fig. 6b discloses the 6.10-fold gradient as an uncorrected upper bound with the matched-control estimate alongside). This is above the standard I usually see at submission.
6. **Cover letter.** One page (~520 words), four pillars matching the abstract's structure, every quantitative claim I spot-checked against the manuscript verified exactly (0/30 vs 36.7%/23.3%; 28.6% vs 35.7–45.2%; ladder 1.04→1.76→1.80 vs 1.07→3.32→2.98, matching SI Section 3.12 including the "below raw JS in 10 of 10 cell classes" claim; 1,750 replicates per background matching Methods). No rebuttal tone, no overselling. Suggested-reviewer paragraph includes an explicit no-conflict statement.

## Major concerns

**M1. Supplementary figure legends are in the wrong file, and the SI contains none.**
All 14 Supplementary Figure legends (Supplementary Figs. 1–14) are printed at the end of the *main manuscript* (after the Correspondence line), while CKI_Supplementary_NC.docx contains zero supplementary figure legends (verified by search) and its TOC lists no figures. NC style places main-figure legends in the manuscript and supplementary figure legends inside the single SI PDF. As submitted, a referee opening the SI cannot interpret any of the 14 supplementary figures without cross-referencing the manuscript file, and production will bounce this. *Severity: major (mandatory formatting fix; trivial to execute — move the 14 legends into the SI, ideally adjacent to a figure index in the TOC).*

**M2. Broken or mis-targeted cross-references (two confirmed).**
- (a) SI Section 3.12 states the Kang batch-1 replication "is shown in main-text Fig. 3d", but main-text Figure 3 has panels a–c only (legend verified); the intended pointer is Fig. 3a. *Severity: major-minor — a broken pointer in a central calibration claim.*
- (b) The main text (Results, pan-cancer section) attributes the exclusion of 32 cell-line-derived aliquots to a "barcode audit (Section 1.7 of the Supplementary Information)", but SI Section 1.7 is the *Probability-Mapping Robustness / Linear-Normalization Re-analysis* section; the barcode audit itself is documented in SI Section 4.3 (TCGA QC) and Supplementary Methods 5.3, with the full audit deferred to the Reproducibility Guide. *Severity: major-minor — the pointer is factually wrong and sits on a data-integrity claim that referees will want to trace.*

**M3. Thirteen of nineteen Supplementary Tables are never cited individually in the main text; "Supplementary Data 1" is uncited anywhere.**
Only Supplementary Tables 1–6 are cited by number in the manuscript body. Tables 7–19 appear only via the collective sentence "All Supplementary Tables (1–19) are provided in CKI_Supplementary_Tables_NC.xlsx" in Data availability. NC requires each supplementary item to be cited; the current state will be flagged at QC. A minimal fix is to add targeted citations at the relevant Results/Methods loci (e.g., Table 7–9 at the MELD/scDist benchmark, Table 10–11 at the drift ladder, Tables 12–17 at the TCGA section, Table 18 at the severity vignettes, Table 19 at the threshold sweep) — the SI already cites them at exactly these loci, so the mapping is mechanical. Additionally, "Supplementary Data 1: Analysis Script Index" appears in the SI TOC and body but is never cited in the manuscript and is absent from the "Supplementary Information is available" statement; either cite it (natural home: Code availability) or fold it into the SI as an unnumbered appendix. *Severity: major (compliance).*

## Minor concerns

1. **SI table-caption order is non-sequential.** Captions are placed at SI-internal first-citation loci, giving the sequence 1, 4, 5, 6, 2, 7–14, 3, 15–19 in both the TOC and the caption block. The numbering itself is correct under MS-first-citation (verified), so this is presentation-only, but production editors prefer caption lists in numerical order; recommend reordering the caption block (and TOC) 1→19 regardless of placement logic.
2. **Mis-pointed Note citation.** Results, candidate-screen paragraph: "inheriting the four-donor structure (Supplementary Note 13)" — Supplementary Note 13 is the Region Glossary (dissection nomenclature); the four-donor structure is treated in Notes 11/12 and Methods 5.7. Re-point. (The second Note-13 citation, for visual-relay/orbitofrontal dissections, is correct.)
3. **Unexplained pair-count shift.** Discussion: "permuting k_n across the 5,151 full-inventory human pairs" versus Results' 4,851 analyzed pairs. Both are internally consistent (C(102,2) vs the 99 filtered entries), but the 5,151 figure appears once without derivation; add a short parenthetical ("all pairs among the 102 unfiltered entries").
4. **Internal P-value ambiguity in the SI.** The WT>KRAS k_n contrast is reported as Dunn–Holm P = 4.2 × 10⁻⁴ in Section 3.13 (and in the main-text Fig. 4 legend), but the clinical-severity section (Note 5 region, SI body) reports "k_n P = 3.4 × 10⁻⁴" without naming the test. If the latter is the Kruskal–Wallis value, label it as such.
5. **Suggested-reviewer optics.** Sten Linnarsson is the senior author of the Siletti atlas that constitutes one of the paper's four pillars; suggesting the primary data producer is not prohibited but is optically weak, and some editors auto-discount such suggestions. Similarly, Fabian Theis is indirectly acknowledged (scanpy developers) — acceptable, but a sixth non-overlapping name would be safer than either.
6. **Table 1 prose order.** The text reads "followed by Neutrophils and NK cells, with Macrophages (n = 15) intermediate", while Table 1 ranks Neutrophil (14.22) < Macrophage (15.49) < NK (17.28); mirroring the table order (or deleting the enumeration) avoids a nitpick.
7. **Two thin legends.** Fig. 5b–d ("ω distribution across all cell-type pairs, showing conserved vs. variable cell types") and Supplementary Fig. 6 ("panel a shows mean ± SD; panel b shows raw pair-level distribution") are below the otherwise high self-containment standard of the other legends; one clause of quantitative content each would fix this.
8. **Consistency of the ex-CC framing.** The phrase "shipped in the LUSC matrix" (Methods) for the 32 LIHC cell-line aliquots is vivid but will read oddly to non-specialists; consider one clarifying clause at first mention.

## Score

**7.5 / 10** (editorial/journal-fit dimension only; scientific soundness deferred to domain reviewers)

Rationale: scope fit, novelty calibration, abstract, word budget, section architecture, reference style, and the renumbered SI apparatus all verify cleanly against the current files — this is a well-prepared submission. The deduction is for the supplementary-apparatus placements and citations (M1–M3), which are non-negotiable at NC QC but mechanically fixable, plus the two broken cross-references sitting on load-bearing claims.
