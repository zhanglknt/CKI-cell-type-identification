## Summary

The manuscript introduces CKI, a Ka/Ks-inspired ratio ω = k_f/k_n of Jensen–Shannon divergences computed on identity genes (numerator) versus housekeeping genes (denominator), as a baseline-normalized index of functional divergence between cell populations. The statistical machinery — one-sided permutation tests with per-iteration gene re-selection, Benjamini–Hochberg FDR within dataset, cluster-aware bootstraps, a block-shuffle null respecting the library × region design, a pseudo-region negative control, and an unusually thorough set of adversarial self-checks (circularity scenarios S1/S2, fixed-panel ablation, k_n-permutation floor, ratio-estimator bias audit, small-cluster coverage study) — is, taken as a whole, well above the field's prevailing standard for this class of method paper. The claims are now appropriately bounded: specificity-first framing, bounded power, scheme-specific absolute scales, and denominator-driven orderings are all disclosed.

My verification of the headline arithmetic all checks out: the BH-resolution argument at m = 31,764 (threshold ≈ 1.6 × 10⁻⁶; ~635 floor P-values; B ≈ 6 × 10⁵) is correct; the 3-of-10 BH survivors among brain classes reproduce exactly from Supplementary Table 5 (astrocytes 0.000999, OPCs 0.001998, committed OPCs 0.004995 pass; fibroblasts 0.02997 fails at rank 4); all TCGA numbers in the main text match Supplementary Tables 12–17; the drift-ladder FPRs match Tables 10–11; the calibration constant 7.70 [6.38, 9.82] matches Section 3.10; the DeLong interval [0.770, 0.838] is validated by a module-seed cluster bootstrap [0.771, 0.836].

The problems I found are concentrated exactly where this revision operated: the global renumbering of Supplementary Notes/Tables. I identified one quantitative claim with no documented source in the cited supplement, one MS/SI numeric mismatch on a headline control, and a cluster of broken cross-references. None threatens the scientific conclusions; all must be fixed before publication.

## Strengths

1. **Null design follows experimental design.** The brain block-shuffle null permutes library-to-region assignments (libraries as blocks), retaining library-level structure while breaking cell–region association, and recomputes pseudobulks, gene selection, ω, and residuals per permutation. This is the correct exchangeability unit, and the pseudo-region negative control (Supplementary Note 12) demonstrates near-nominal marginal tail rates (5.79%/6.87% vs 5% nominal) with full power on same-origin pairs (37.6%).
2. **Gene-selection circularity is handled in the null, not ignored.** Identity genes are re-selected at every permutation under the same per-pair rule (reselect_identity = True), and the anti-conservative legacy fixed-panel variant (TCGA per-cancer test) is explicitly quarantined as uninformative rather than silently reported.
3. **Uncertainty quantification is cluster-aware at the right level.** Region-clustered block bootstrap for landscape quantities, entry-clustered bootstrap for Tabula Sapiens correlations, sample-level cluster bootstrap for TCGA ratios, donor cluster bootstrap for the brain gradient, and a Monte-Carlo coverage study (Supplementary Note 2) justifying studentized bootstrap-t replacement for the 6–7-cluster classes (coverage 0.953/0.951 vs 0.876/0.873 percentile). The pseudo-replicated 300-split calibration interval is explicitly superseded by a two-stage population bootstrap.
4. **Multiple-testing reporting is honest.** The permutation-resolution floor at m = 31,764 (min q = 0.520) is correctly framed as a resolution statement, with the raw-P excess calibrated against the design-matched null of the count itself (P = 0.011; 0.022 Bonferroni across two tails).
5. **Adversarial self-falsification.** The S1/S2 circularity scenarios (Supplementary Note 1), the k_n-permutation floor (0.524 vs observed 0.089; Supplementary Note 5), and the fixed-panel ablation (Supplementary Note 9) quantify the method's blind spots rather than hiding them.

## Major Issues

**M1. A quantitative robustness claim has no documented source in the cited supplement.**
Discussion (¶ "The candidate screen illustrates a second principle…") states "only 3 of 39 are retained under a global-k_n estimator (Supplementary Note 14)", and Results (¶ "But the set is estimator-sensitive…") repeats "only 3 of 39 Strong candidates survive a switch to a global-k_n estimator". I searched the full Supplementary text: Supplementary Note 14 ("Brain set-level enrichment of the block-shuffle signal") contains the tier-graded raw-P enrichment, the thalamo-temporal axis analysis, and the microglia composition null — but no global-k_n estimator analysis and no "3 of 39" statement anywhere in the SI (the global-k_n variant appears only in Section 3.5 and Supplementary Note 11, as rank-correlation summaries ρ = 0.142 and ρ = 0.09, with no candidate-retention count). This number is load-bearing: it is the stated reason the catalogue is "strictly hypothesis-generating". Either add the analysis (which note, which script, which output file) or delete the claim from both locations. *Severity: Major — unverifiable quantitative claim, easy to fix.*

**M2. Cross-reference integrity after the global SI renumbering is broken in at least five places.**
This is precisely the class of error a reordering pass creates, and it needs a systematic re-audit, not piecemeal fixes:
(a) **Table numbering does not follow the stated first-citation order.** First citations in the main text are: Table 1 (Results ¶2), Tables 2 and 3 (TCGA ¶ "Four controls…"), Table 4 (cross-organ ¶), Table 5 (brain ¶), Table 6 (screen ¶). The SI Table of Contents and body order is 1, 4, 5, 6, 2, 7–11, 12–14, 3, 15–19 — i.e., Tables 2 and 3 are numbered after Tables 4–6 despite being cited earlier.
(b) **Supplementary Tables 7–19 are never cited in the main text** (grep of the full main text: only Tables 1–6 appear; the only other mention is the collective "Supplementary Tables (1–19)" in Data availability). NC style requires each supplementary table to be cited in the main text.
(c) Supplementary Table 14's legend says "Main-text Fig. 5b-d" — the LUAD pairwise contrasts are Fig. 4b–d; Fig. 5 is the cross-organ figure.
(d) SI Section 3.12 (Kang batch-1 paragraph) says "the replication is shown in main-text Fig. 3d" — Fig. 3 has panels a–c only; this should be Fig. 3a.
(e) Main text (TCGA ¶) attributes the barcode audit to "Section 1.7 of the Supplementary Information" — Section 1.7 is the probability-mapping robustness section; the audit is documented in Section 5.3 and the Reproducibility Guide.
(f) Main text (screen ¶) cites Supplementary Note 13 for "inheriting the four-donor structure" — Note 13 is the region glossary and contains no donor-structure discussion (the relevant material is in Note 12 / Section 5.7).
*Severity: Major (collectively) — factual pointer errors in a revision whose stated purpose was renumbering consistency.*

**M3. Main text and SI attach the same donor-bootstrap CIs to different point estimates for the headline matched controls.**
Main text (brain ¶): "a span-matched control … yields 3.68 (donor-level bootstrap [1.67, 3.97])" and "a combined span- and size-matched control yields 3.66 (donor-level bootstrap [1.92, 3.78])"; Fig. 6 legend repeats "3.66-fold (donor-level cluster bootstrap 95% CI [1.92, 3.78])". Supplementary Note 11, however, reports the donor-level cluster bootstrap point estimates as **3.28** for both controls ("the same donor cluster bootstrap … gives a ratio-of-means gradient of 3.28 [1.67, 3.97]"; "a donor-level median of 3.28 [1.92, 3.78]"), with 3.68/3.66 being the observed (non-resampled) values. As written, the MS pairs each CI with a point estimate that does not correspond to that interval's sampling scheme. Harmonize the presentation (e.g., "observed 3.66; donor-bootstrap median 3.28, 95% CI [1.92, 3.78]"). *Severity: Major — numeric inconsistency on a headline robustness quantity.*

## Minor Issues

1. **SI body describes only 4 of 19 tables.** The SI document carries description paragraphs for Supplementary Tables 1, 4, 5, 6 only; Tables 2, 3, and 7–19 appear solely as TOC entries (their legends live in row 1 of the xlsx sheets). Either include all legends in the SI document or none.
2. **Empty heading.** The SI ends with the heading "Supplementary Data 1: Analysis Script Index" followed by no content.
3. **Package version stamp inconsistency.** The SI refers to "the released cki package (v0.5.4)" in three places (Algorithm-1 note, Section 3.8, Section 5.1), while the main text reports v0.5.5 throughout (Code availability, Methods ¶ "package parity").
4. **Post-hoc stratified family.** The intra-cerebellar Bergmann-glia family (m = 21, within-family min q = 0.042) is the only surviving stratified family, but the number of stratified families examined is not stated and no selection adjustment is applied across them. Given the hypothesis-generating framing this is acceptable, but the family count should be disclosed.
5. **Multiplicity of the LUAD adjusted component tests.** The admixture/smoking-adjusted OLS contrasts (Supplementary Tables 16–17) are reported uncorrected across 3 contrasts × 3 metrics × 4 covariate models. The ω-level KRAS–WT contrast is robust to any reasonable correction (P = 3.3 × 10⁻⁴), but the k_f "functional component" (adjusted P = 0.009) would not survive a strict 9-test family-wise correction (~0.08). The Dunn–Holm P = 0.015 is corrected only within its 3-contrast family. A sentence acknowledging the family definition would suffice.
6. **Abstract calibration of the pan-cancer claim.** The abstract's "consistent pan-cancer pattern" is direction-consistent in 5/5 cancer types, but the cluster-bootstrap CI excludes 1 in only 4/5, and LIHC is mapping-sensitive (linear 1.11 [0.94, 1.30] vs softmax 1.29 [1.09, 1.53]; Supplementary Table 2). Consider "(CI excluding 1 in four of five)" in the abstract for precision.
7. **FDR family for the 4,851 Tabula Sapiens per-pair tests is implicit.** Statistics and reproducibility defines group-level families (m = 17 human cell types) but does not state whether the 4,851 pair-level permutation P-values were BH-corrected as a 4,851-test family. One sentence.
8. **Residual circularity of ω_cal (disclosed but worth one explicit sentence).** The 7.70 baseline and the permutation null internalize the same per-pair gene-selection bias, so ω_cal is an estimator-internal rescaling, not an externally validated scale. The text already hedges (one significant digit, dataset-relative, brain-internal 9.73); an explicit "estimator-internal" statement would close the loop.
9. **T2/T3 drift-ladder tiers use B = 30 per-pair nulls** (resolution 1/31), so the tier FPRs (90.9%, 84.8%) carry several-percentage-point Monte-Carlo noise. Disclosed in Section 5.10, but tier rates should carry Wilson or MC-error intervals as Table 10 does for Kang.

**7.0 / 10**

The required fixes are documentary: restore one unsourced claim (M1), re-audit cross-references after the renumbering (M2), and harmonize the matched-control point estimates with their bootstrap CIs (M3).
